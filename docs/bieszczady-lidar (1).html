Known abandoned villages

Approximate locations. Most were emptied in 1944–1947 (deportations, Operation Vistula).

What to look for

  • Rectangular cellar pits and house platforms
  • Field terraces and boundary banks on slopes
  • Sunken roads (holloways) leading to nothing
  • Church or cemetery outlines, often on a small rise
  • Straight drainage ditches in flat valley floors

Best seen at zoom 16–18. The relief layer only loads from about zoom 12.
