# 07 - Project decisions

Decisions made by the project owner. These override conflicting text in 05-synthesis.md and 06-critique.md.

## D1. Desk-only scope (2026-11-13)
The project re-uses existing public data (LiDAR, orthophotos, historical maps, registers) for discovery. It does **not** touch the ground: no fieldwork, metal detecting, test pits or collecting.

Consequences:
- Heritage-permit questions (Monuments Act art. 36 ust. 1 pkt 12, art. 109c, and the app-registration regime in force from 2027-01-01 per Dz.U. 2023 poz. 1904 as postponed by Dz.U. 2025 poz. 537) do not apply. They can be dropped from the main plan and kept only as a footnote.
- National-park access rules (BdPN) are irrelevant to our work.
- Drop the field-sample kill-criterion and "field-checkable village" requirement from 06 §revised plan. Validation must be desk-based: 1944 aerial photo, 1850s cadastre, PRG precinct polygons, independent counts (1921 census building numbers), a second independent interpreter, and red-team checks of the known-check.
- No candidate is ever labelled "field-confirmed". The strongest claim is "LiDAR feature consistent with N independent historical sources".
- Field verification, if it ever happens, is for others (e.g. researchers or the conservator) to do with their own permits. Our output can support that.

Still in scope (not permit issues, but they matter at the dissemination stage): what precise coordinates we publish (looting risk), how we handle cemeteries and graves, and the GUGiK licence terms for derived products.
